Selected projects

Research

Principal Investigator · Completed

AI-based Service Request Analysis and Rate Limiting

Led an R&D project (RP #N-IS1-25-0006) for the National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA) on fair and efficient use of national service resources. Built an unsupervised anomaly detection model over engineered behavioural features from roughly 671,000 service requests, and designed a behaviour-driven rate-limiting framework that maps continuous anomaly scores to graduated risk controls. The retrainable framework, trained models, and validation pipeline were delivered to NADRA in November 2025.

Co-Principal Investigator

Careem Captain Fraud Detection

Explored data processing techniques and machine learning algorithms to automate fraud detection for Careem Captains (drivers). Careem is a leading ride-hailing provider in the Middle East.

Co-supervised

Solving PDEs with Physics-Informed Neural Networks

Developed and trained physics-informed neural networks for solving partial differential equations with a graduate student at GIK Institute. Improved PINN performance using Lie symmetry groups, and solved the Newell–Whitehead–Segel, Allen–Cahn, and Burgers' equations.

Journal article

Detecting Hidden Explosives and Narcotics

Developed a deep neural network approach for the rapid detection of hidden explosives and narcotics using multipulse Nuclear Quadrupole Resonance (NQR).

MS thesis

Neural Networks for Medical Image Segmentation

Developed and trained deep neural networks for medical image segmentation during my master's research, and collaborated with fellow graduate students on integrating active contour models into neural networks for medical image processing.

Book chapter

Skin Cancer Detection with CNNs and Vision Transformers

Applied transfer learning to fine-tune convolutional neural networks and vision transformers for the classification of cancerous skin lesion images.
